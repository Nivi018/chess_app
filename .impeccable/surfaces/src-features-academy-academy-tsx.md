# Mi avance — extension contract

Mode: Operate. Extend the existing academy surface; keep the established shell, sage palette, system typography and UI wrappers. The user chose a joint summary and an explained next action, not a level score.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Turn saved attempts into an understandable next step, not a gamified score or a wall of metric cards.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the academy's teal, sage, paper surfaces, fine rules and compact controls. No changes to the global visual identity.

STORY: The learner opens Mi avance, sees lesson and review completion separately, understands the suggestion, and opens that exact activity. Unknown historical results remain unknown.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Keep the existing header and navigation. In the main area, show Mi avance, a short privacy note, one next-step band with a teal action, then two ruled completion summaries. A pair of readable activity lists follows, becoming a single column on mobile. No charts or decorative icons.

FORM: Local extension of the established learning surface; no concept tournament applies. Recommendation prioritizes pending lessons, pending reviews, then a completed review whose first result needed help. Its reason is always visible.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
